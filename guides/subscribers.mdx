---
title: "Subscribers"
description: "Email notifications for status updates"
---

Subscribers receive email notifications when incidents are created, updated, or resolved.

## Subscription flow

1. User enters email on your status page
2. Confirmation email sent (double opt-in)
3. User clicks confirmation link
4. Subscriber is now active

## Notification types

- **Incident created** — New incident reported
- **Incident updated** — Status change or new update
- **Incident resolved** — Issue resolved
- **Component down** — Service disruption detected
- **Component recovered** — Service back online

## Component filtering

Subscribers can choose to receive notifications for all components or only specific ones.
