title: "Status Pages"
description: "Create and manage public status pages"
---

A status page is the public-facing page where your users check system status.

## Features

- **Custom subdomain** — `your-company.axonstatus.com`
- **Custom domain** — `status.your-company.com` via CNAME
- **Themes** — Light and dark mode
- **Timezone** — Display times in your preferred timezone
- **Search engines** — Option to hide from indexing

## Creating a status page

In the dashboard, go to **Status Pages** and click **Create Page**. You'll need:

- **Name** — Your company or service name
- **Slug** — URL-friendly identifier (e.g., `acme`)

## Settings

Configure your page under the status page tabs:

| Tab | Settings |
|-----|----------|
| **General** | Name, slug, website URL, logo, timezone |
| **Components** | Add and organize service components |
| **Domain** | Subdomain and custom domain setup |
| **Appearance** | Theme, incident history style, status colors |
| **Widget** | Embeddable status badge |
