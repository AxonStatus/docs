## Core features

- **Status pages** — Public pages showing your system status
- **Components** — Track individual services and infrastructure
- **Incidents** — Communicate outages and issues to your users
- **Monitoring** — Automated uptime checks (HTTP, TCP, DNS, Ping)
- **Subscribers** — Email notifications for status updates
- **Widget & Badge** — Embed your status anywhere
